About Somya

A chart is a map, not a verdict.

Namaste, I’m Somya. I help people make sense of their birth charts, so that big decisions about love, work and timing feel less like guesses and more like choices.

My readings are honest, practical and kind. I explain every planet and house in simple words, and I always pair what the chart shows with steps you can act on.

On Instagram and YouTube, as Somya Samvaad, I talk through astrology the same way: as a conversation.
